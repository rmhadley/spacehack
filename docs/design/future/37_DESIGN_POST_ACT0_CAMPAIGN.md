# DESIGN: Post-Act-0 Campaign — Epilogue Choice, the Blockade, the Derelict, Act 2

**Status: FUTURE — user roadmap captured 2026-09-04, rulings pending.**
Active campaign: doc 36 (Bar). This doc records the arc after Act 0
and the prison arc: the epilogue branch, Act 1's blockade and derelict
dive, and the start of Act 2. The existing post-escape content is
DISCARDED — the user does not want to keep the little stuff that
exists there today.

## The arc (user's words, lightly organized)

1. Escape Mars with the deep-prison data in hand.
2. **The epilogue choice**:
   - **Please your faction** — hand over the data, get an
     faction-appropriate reward. Merchants: a return on the 8,000cr
     bond investment (stated). The other three need ideas (below).
   - **Keep everything to yourself** — a harder path from here on,
     with twists at the end (user has these in mind).
3. **Act 1: past the Luyten blockade** — multiple paths to do so.
4. **The prison-like intermission**: use the Mars-prison knowledge to
   UNMASK a giant alien derelict ship beyond the blockade — the thing
   the militia scanners were detecting when the blockade went up.
5. **Dive the giant alien ship** (as we dived the prison). Emerge
   with alien tech — specifically an **alien jump drive**: it lets us
   go places we shouldn't go.
6. **Act 2 begins** when we use the drive and jump to the systems the
   prisoner devoured.

## Initial thoughts (nothing ruled on)

### Faction rewards — the epilogue payoff

Each reward should pay off its chain's fiction AND be load-bearing in
Act 1 (the rewards can BE the blockade paths):

- **Merchants** (settled): the bond pays out — the Guild buys the
  data at a return on the 8,000cr. Credits + standing.
- **Militia** (idea): rank has its privileges — a **militia
  transponder / blockade clearance**. The chain was containment
  thinking; the reward is being on the right side of the cordon. This
  is also the OFFICIAL path past Luyten: waved through, but watched.
- **Bar** (idea): the wolf barkeep's network provides a **scrubbed
  false transponder** — the sneak path past the blockade, plus fence
  access for everything Act 1 loots. The underworld paid its debt.
- **Lab** (idea): the researchers turn the prison data into a
  **sensor/analysis suite refit** — deeper scans, anomaly detection at
  range. Which is exactly the tech that (fiction-wise) helps UNMASK
  the derelict later: the Lab's reward is how you see the thing the
  blockade is hiding. Science path: detect and slip the patrols.

If all four rewards double as blockade paths, the epilogue choice
stays meaningful for the whole act — and the KEEP-IT-TO-YOURSELF
branch has no pass at all: harder, as specified.

### The keep-everything branch

Harder road (no faction pass, no reward, heat from all sides), with
the user's end-twists. Design space noted, not decided: unaffiliated
carriers of the prison data may read/activate alien systems the
factions cannot — the lone-wolf edge that pays off at the derelict or
in Act 2.

### The blockade (Act 1 gate)

Luyten already exists as a place (blockade/blockade_south planets,
the militia inspector from mil_q3). Making the cordon the act gate
reuses established geography. Multiple paths: the four faction passes
(above), plus at least one no-faction option (the long way / the
bribe / the run) so the keep-it branch and free players aren't
walled.

### The derelict dive

Same shape as the prison descent: a big authored interior, descended
in stages, emerged-from with the act's artifact. The uncloaking beat
gives the blockade its REASON — the militia scanners were detecting
the derelict all along. (Nice retrofit: the alien signals the prison
radiated, the Mars door, and the scanner pings become one thread.)

### The jump drive → Act 2

"Places we shouldn't go" = the devoured systems. The drive is the
act-2 gate mechanic: unlocks systems outside the jump network. Act 2
opens the moment it's first used.

### System considerations (for doc 33's quest-polish backlog)

- The epilogue choice is a branch INSIDE a completed chain — the
  prologue lock-in chose the chain; this chooses its disposition.
  Needs: two mutually-exclusive reward steps keyed on a disposition
  flag (delivered | kept), save-safe.
- Blockade paths need per-run gating on faction + epilogue
  disposition + the chosen reward item.
- The devoured systems need a solar-system spec pattern for
  "unreachable until drive" — likely a new reachability class, not
  per-system hacks.
