## First-pass shape (for review)

**The geography is already authored.** The restricted sector (6x6,
east side of luyten_star at ~183,62, behind the picket wall) IS
the anomaly's address. "Past the Line" = the east side of the
system the player has spent the whole act learning to enter. No
new system required for the act 1 finale — the payoff happens
where the placeholder already sits.

**The unveiling (doc 37's intermission).** The Militia blockaded
because their scanners detected something. The player's alien
tech (the archive / gate tech / kept data) unveils what they were
seeing: a giant derelict alien ship at the restricted sector.
Design questions: does it literally uncloak/appear? Was it always
visible east of the wall and simply unreachable? (Lean: always
THERE — the scanners detected it; the player's tech lets them
SEE it / reach it — the quarantine fiction stays honest.)

**The dive.** Prison-pattern: a giant alien derelict explored in
descending floors — the act's capstone dungeon. It pays in alien
tech; specifically the alien jump drive (doc 37: "lets us go
places we shouldn't go") — the door to Act 2's devoured systems.

**Arrival placements (from the methods):**
- Checkpoint crossings: the far-side entry zone (west face of the
  restricted sector area?)
- The hidden gate: the far-EAST edge of the map, at a
  jump-point-typical position, no gate there (materialize from
  nowhere)

**The jump drive = Act 2's door, not Act 1 content.** Using it
first (jumping to a devoured system) opens Act 2. Act 1 ends at
the drive in hand.

## Open questions (the review agenda)

1. The derelict's visibility fiction: always-there-but-unreachable
   vs. unveiled by tech? (Lean: always there.)
2. How does the player physically reach/enter it — dock like a
   derelict (bump → board)? A special approach?
3. Scale: how many floors / what structure? (Prison was 5 — the
   finale should feel GIANT.)
4. What's aboard besides the drive — legendary loot, lore (feeds
   doc 42), the prisoner's history?
5. Does the Militia react to the player at the derelict (the
   intermission fiction: their scanners watch this space)?
6. Act 2 door: is the first jump drive use a scene/milestone?
