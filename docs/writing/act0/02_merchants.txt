### step.mer_q1_contract.title — TITLE
Sign the Contract

### step.mer_q1_contract.description — DESCRIPTION
Sign the Guild's contract on Earth. It grants the Guild first access to anything recovered from the Mars site, and grants you the cutter that may open it. The first clause sends you to an old Wolf 359 claim - because every discovery begins with someone insisting they got there first.

### step.mer_q1_contract.dialogue.guild_master.intro — DIALOGUE (Guild Master) intro (before the step is accepted)
The contract is simple because complicated contracts make people read them. The Guild gets first access to salvage and data from the Mars site; you get a cutter tuned to the door's stress signature. Sign, and we begin with the Wolf 359 escrow ore that will pay for the work.

### step.mer_q1_contract.dialogue.guild_master.active — DIALOGUE (Guild Master) active (while the step is in progress)
The contract is filed. Give the Guild time to move the deed through three offices that all claim not to know us. Once it clears, go to Wolf 359 b. The caves beneath the listening post hold ore the Guild has been defending on paper for years.

### step.mer_q1_contract.dialogue.guild_master.complete — DIALOGUE (Guild Master) complete (after the step is done)
The deed is yours to enforce. Recover the ore from the Wolf 359 b caves before the consortium turns our legal victory into a physical one.

### step.mer_q1_contract.dialogue.guild_master.option_label — DIALOGUE (Guild Master) menu option row

### step.mer_q1_contract.completion_flavor — COMPLETION FLAVOR (what happens when this step is done)
The Guild has filed the deed and transferred the claim into your name. When the clearance arrives, go to Wolf 359 b and secure the escrow ore before the rival consortium does. A legal claim is only as strong as the person standing on it.

### step.mer_q1_contract.ready_message — READY MESSAGE (the summons that begins this step)
The claim is cleared. Reach Wolf 359 b and recover the escrow ore from the caves beneath the listening post. The consortium knows the deed changed hands; expect them to contest it with more than paperwork.

### step.mer_q2_strike.title — TITLE
The Claim

### step.mer_q2_strike.description — DESCRIPTION
Descend into the Wolf 359 b surface caves and stake the Guild's abandoned prospecting claim. Rival prospectors from a competing consortium got there first - clear them out and secure the rare earth metals.

### step.mer_q2_strike.dialogue.guild_master.intro — DIALOGUE (Guild Master) intro (before the step is accepted)
The claim has been dormant long enough for everyone to forget who paid for it. The ore should still be in the deep cache, unless the consortium has decided a neglected deed is an invitation. Get down there and secure it before they do.

### step.mer_q2_strike.dialogue.guild_master.active — DIALOGUE (Guild Master) active (while the step is in progress)
The claim is at Wolf 359 b. The listening post staff will not ask questions because questions are bad for business. The consortium's prospectors will ask them anyway.

### step.mer_q2_strike.dialogue.guild_master.complete — DIALOGUE (Guild Master) complete (after the step is done)
You got the ore out. It is raw, but the assay is already making people nervous. Take it to the specialist at Tau Ceti b; he can smelt it without asking the material to become ordinary.

### step.mer_q2_strike.completion_flavor — COMPLETION FLAVOR (what happens when this step is done)
The raw ore is loaded into your hold - valuable, disputed, and useless until it is smelted. The Guild's only specialist for material this pure works at Tau Ceti b.

### step.mer_q3_transport.title — TITLE
The Transport

### step.mer_q3_transport.description — DESCRIPTION
The ore is in your mission hold, unrefined and already disputed. Take it to the Guild's salvage specialist at Tau Ceti b. Rival ships are watching the route; to them, this is not a delivery but evidence that the Guild still intends to own the future.

### step.mer_q3_transport.dialogue.salvage_specialist.intro — DIALOGUE (Salvage Specialist) intro (before the step is accepted)
Word of the abandoned claim reached me months ago. If the Guild finally found the ore, I want my cut. Hand it over and I will fire up the smelter - assuming it agrees to run.

### step.mer_q3_transport.dialogue.salvage_specialist.active — DIALOGUE (Salvage Specialist) active (while the step is in progress)
The smelter is running hot and the readings refuse to settle. Give it time. If the alloy behaves, it will be unlike anything the Guild has sold before.

### step.mer_q3_transport.dialogue.salvage_specialist.complete — DIALOGUE (Salvage Specialist) complete (after the step is done)
The smelt is complete. The alloy is stronger than anything I have worked with, and it keeps returning the same pulse. Take it to Vega and recover the calibration data before the consortium strips the wreck.

### step.mer_q3_transport.dialogue.salvage_specialist.option_label — DIALOGUE (Salvage Specialist) menu option row

### step.mer_q3_transport.completion_flavor — COMPLETION FLAVOR (what happens when this step is done)
The specialist feeds the ore into the smelter and goes quiet. The assay returns impossible purity, with traces that do not match any human mine. 'Give me time,' he says. 'If this is what I think it is, the cutter will be the least valuable thing we make from it.'

### step.mer_q3_transport.ready_message — READY MESSAGE (the summons that begins this step)
The smelt is complete. The alloy is stronger than anything in the Guild's catalogue, and it carries a repeating stress pattern like a recorded pulse. Collect it at Tau Ceti b, then recover calibration data from a derelict near Vega. The consortium has put raiders on the wreck; they would rather destroy the evidence than let us learn what the material can do.

### step.mer_q4_calibrate.description — DESCRIPTION

### step.mer_q4_calibrate.dialogue.salvage_specialist.active — DIALOGUE (Salvage Specialist) active (while the step is in progress)
The alloy is in your hold. The wreck is near Vega's observation deck. Fight through the consortium raiders, board the derelict, and recover the calibration data. Without it, the Guild cannot tell whether the cutter is opening a door or answering a call.

### step.mer_q5_cutter.title — TITLE
The Cutter

### step.mer_q5_cutter.description — DESCRIPTION
Return to the Guild Master on Earth with the smelted alloy and the calibration data. The cutter can be assembled - and the door on Mars can finally be opened.

### step.mer_q5_cutter.dialogue.guild_master.intro — DIALOGUE (Guild Master) intro (before the step is accepted)
